Solar Energy Harnessing Project

In this project, we explore solar energy harnessing using fixed and tracking systems. Fixed panels, as shown in Fig. 1.1, remain static, while tracking systems adjust to follow the sun’s path, maximizing energy capture.

We aim to optimize tracking precision, boost energy efficiency, enhance durability, and integrate smart features for a sustainable solution in Nairobi.

Quick Facts

  • Location: Nairobi, Africa
  • Tracking: Dual-Axis System
  • Energy Gain: Up to 40% more
